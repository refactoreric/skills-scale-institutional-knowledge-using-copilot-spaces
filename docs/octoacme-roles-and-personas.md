# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads design and execute quality assurance strategies to ensure features meet acceptance criteria and maintain product reliability. They collaborate with Product Managers, Developers, and Release Managers to define testability requirements and validate quality before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and test plan aligned with acceptance criteria
- Create and maintain automated test suites
- Conduct manual testing and validate acceptance criteria
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigations
- Work with Developers on testability during design and implementation
- Coordinate QA sign-off before release
- Triage and track defects through resolution
- Partner with Project Managers to ensure quality gates are met before milestones

### Goals
- Ensure features meet quality standards and customer expectations
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Enable fast, confident deployments

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and kickoff (define test approach)
- Daily standups (test status and blockers)
- PR reviews (quality gates and test coverage)
- Pre-release readiness reviews
- Post-incident retrospectives

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects own the technical vision and design decisions that guide the implementation of features and systems. They work closely with Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers to ensure scalability, performance, and maintainability while managing technical risks.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach and system architecture for major features
- Lead design reviews and technical decision-making
- Identify and assess technical risks and dependencies
- Mentor Developers on best practices and code quality
- Evaluate technology choices and trade-offs
- Collaborate with Product Managers on technical feasibility and timelines
- Document technical decisions and rationale in architecture records

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and performant systems
- Minimize technical debt and complexity
- Reduce cycle time through effective system design

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Technical risk assessments at project kickoff and planning
- Code review guidance and mentorship
- Dependency mapping with other teams
- Post-release performance and observability reviews

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove blockers, and coach teams on process improvements. They work with Project Managers and the delivery team to maintain a healthy, high-performing team culture and continuous improvement mindset.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove impediments and blockers that slow team progress
- Coach the team on agile principles and practices
- Track team velocity and sprint metrics
- Help the team identify and implement process improvements
- Coordinate with Project Managers on schedule and timeline risks
- Foster psychological safety and open communication

### Goals
- Enable the team to self-organize and deliver incrementally
- Continuously improve team velocity and quality
- Maintain a healthy, engaged team culture

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-one coaching conversations
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Metrics reviews with Project Managers
- Process improvement recommendations

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, strategic direction, and resource support for projects. They collaborate with Product Managers and Project Managers to ensure alignment with business goals and maintain visibility into project progress.

### Responsibilities
- Define business priorities and strategic goals
- Provide approval and resource support for projects
- Communicate project status and impact to executive leadership
- Identify and resolve cross-functional dependencies
- Provide feedback on features and outcomes
- Review and approve release decisions
- Support escalations and risk mitigation

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain alignment between product delivery and business strategy
- Reduce organizational risk and bottlenecks

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based stakeholder updates
- Project kickoff and approval meetings
- Risk escalations and decision reviews
- Release announcements and impact reporting
- Executive dashboards and business metrics

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment planning, release readiness, and post-release verification. They work with Developers, QA/Testing Leads, and Project Managers to ensure smooth, low-risk releases to production while maintaining communication with stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain release plans and deployment schedules
- Coordinate pre-release checks and smoke tests
- Manage release notes and communications
- Execute deployments and monitor post-deployment health
- Coordinate rollback plans and incident response
- Track release metrics and document lessons learned
- Communicate deployment status to stakeholders and support teams

### Goals
- Deliver releases on schedule with minimal risk
- Enable fast recovery from deployment issues
- Maintain clear visibility into release status and outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and readiness reviews
- Pre-deployment coordination with QA and Developers
- Release notes and stakeholder announcements
- Post-deployment verification and incident communication
- Deployment runbooks and retrospectives

---

## Product Operations

### Role Summary
Product Operations teams manage roadmap analytics, metrics tracking, and product performance monitoring. They work with Product Managers, Project Managers, and Developers to measure outcomes, identify trends, and inform data-driven decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Track and report on key product metrics and KPIs
- Analyze product usage patterns and user behavior
- Maintain product roadmap and release analytics
- Support success metrics measurement from Project One-pagers
- Create dashboards and reports for stakeholders
- Identify trends and opportunities for optimization
- Collaborate with teams to define and track success criteria

### Goals
- Enable data-driven decision-making across the organization
- Measure and communicate product impact and value
- Support continuous improvement through insights and analytics

### Typical Communication
- Weekly metrics reviews with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Dashboards and analytics reports for stakeholders
- Success metrics tracking and outcome reporting
- Insights and trend analysis
- Post-release impact and performance reviews
